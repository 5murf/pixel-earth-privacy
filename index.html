Pixel Earth Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-20

Pixel Earth ("the app") is a walking game. You walk, earn Ink from your steps, and paint map tiles.

What we collect

  • Precise location (GPS). Used while the app is open, to show you on the map and to check that a tile is within reach when you paint. The app does not read your location when it is closed.
  • Step count. Read from your phone's step sensor to earn Ink. This also works while the app is closed, so steps you walk then still count. Only the step total is used, never your location.
  • Account id. A random guest id made on your phone. If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple, we also store the account's unique id from that provider. We do not receive your password.
  • Username. Only if you set one. Other players and the admin can see it.
  • Painted tiles. Which tiles you painted, their colour and when.

Where it is stored

On the developer's own server. Your game data is not sold.

The app shows optional rewarded ads, which you can watch to get extra Ink. Ads are served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID and other device data to show and measure ads. See Google's privacy policy. There are no other third-party analytics in the app.

Your last known position is also kept on your phone so the map opens quickly.

Sharing

Your username and the tiles you painted are visible to other players on the map. Your exact location is not shared with other players. Google and Apple only see sign-in requests if you use their sign-in.

Your choices and deletion

You can play as a guest without signing in. To have your account and data deleted, email privacy@smurf.cc with your username or account id. We delete it within 30 days. Uninstalling the app removes the data on your phone.

Children

The app is not aimed at children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page and its date if this policy changes.
